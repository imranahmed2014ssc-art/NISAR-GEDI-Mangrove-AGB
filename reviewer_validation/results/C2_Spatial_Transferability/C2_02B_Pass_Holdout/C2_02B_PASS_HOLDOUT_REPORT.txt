DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Track-group proxy: complete GEDI L4A source_file/acquisition pass.
All observations from one acquisition pass were withheld together.
The held-out source file was absent from the active training data.
A 500-m spatial exclusion buffer was additionally applied to training observations.
Frozen model family: Extra Trees.
Frozen predictor family: full 14P multiscale predictor set.
Hyperparameters were retuned only inside each active training sample using
four-fold GroupKFold grouped by 3-km cv_block_id.
No C2 model-family or predictor-family selection occurred.
