INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These variables characterize broad hydrometeorological scene conditions.
They do not provide a local tidal-stage measurement at individual GEDI footprints.
Therefore tide remains an unresolved scene-level confounder unless a reliable independent tide record or tide model is added.
Because only two NISAR scenes are available, differences in rainfall, soil moisture, runoff, temperature, acquisition season, and radar response cannot be causally separated.
This analysis is intended to document environmental differences between the June and September sensitivity scenes, not to claim hydrological causation of model error.
