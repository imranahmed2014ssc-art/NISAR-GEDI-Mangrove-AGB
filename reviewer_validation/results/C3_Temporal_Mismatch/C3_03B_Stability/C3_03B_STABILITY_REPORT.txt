DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each GEDI observation was screened using a 15-m-radius footprint buffer.
Baseline optical conditions were represented by the trailing 365 days ending at the GEDI acquisition date of its source-file pass.
Follow-up conditions were represented by the trailing 365 days ending at the Sep-17-2026 primary NISAR acquisition.
Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized supplied median NDVI and NBR.
Dynamic World supplied mean tree, flooded vegetation, and water probabilities.
tree + flooded_vegetation probability was used as a combined mangrove-relevant vegetation probability.

FROZEN SCREENING THRESHOLDS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
|delta NDVI| >= 0.15
|delta NBR| >= 0.15
|delta Dynamic World (trees + flooded vegetation)| >= 0.25
|delta Dynamic World water| >= 0.25
Minimum valid optical observations per window = 3

These are conservative study-specific screening cut-offs, not independently validated ecological disturbance thresholds.
