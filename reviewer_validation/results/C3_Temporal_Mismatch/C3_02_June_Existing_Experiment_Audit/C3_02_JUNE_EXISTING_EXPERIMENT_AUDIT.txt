INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This audit does not reinterpret the June experiment as a controlled temporal experiment.
June 25 and September 17 differ not only in GEDI-to-NISAR temporal gap but also in acquisition date, season, hydrology, moisture, tide, and potentially other scene conditions.
Therefore any similar model performance can support only the statement that the tested 84-day reduction did not materially alter nonlinear model performance.
It cannot establish that temporal mismatch is irrelevant.
