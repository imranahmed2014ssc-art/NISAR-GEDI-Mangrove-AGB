INTERPRETATION RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. G1 contains substantially more observations than G4; stratum metrics therefore have unequal precision.
2. Per-pass gap associations use only eight unequal acquisition units and are descriptive.
3. A monotonic deterioration with larger gap would support temporal mismatch as a performance limitation.
4. Absence of such a trend would NOT prove temporal mismatch irrelevant because gap is confounded with acquisition date, season, geography, hydrology, and possible disturbance.
5. The existing June-2026 scene must therefore still be evaluated separately after this step.
