DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Five geographic outer folds were created and frozen before C1 reviewer-validation modeling.
For every outer fold, all model-family selection, predictor-family selection, preprocessing, and hyperparameter tuning occurred inside the buffered outer-training sample only.
The outer test file was not opened by this script until the complete inner selection procedure for that fold was finished.
Seven predictor families and seven historical model families were predeclared.
Inner tuning used four-fold GroupKFold by 3-km cv_block_id.
Selection criterion: minimum mean inner RMSE.
