**Description:**
Versioned review/submission reproducibility package for the Rev6.4 NISAR-GEDI mangrove AGBD manuscript. The archive contains the input-granule manifest, feature-extraction and validation code, software-environment record, frozen fold memberships, primary and conditional footprint-level out-of-fold predictions, C15 alternative-spatial-baseline sensitivity outputs, sample-flow and attrition summaries, diagnostic figures, regeneration guidance for the principal manuscript tables and figures, and SHA-256 checksums. Raw NISAR, GEDI, and Global Mangrove Watch source products are not redistributed and must be obtained from the original data providers.
